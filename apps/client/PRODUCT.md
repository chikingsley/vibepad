# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

VibePad serves developers who run several coding-agent harnesses across projects
and computers and want one dependable place to supervise the work. The primary
workflow happens on a landscape iPad with a keyboard and Apple Pencil: move
among active sessions, understand current state, inspect files and changes,
review a live product surface, give visual or spoken direction, and coordinate
agents or shared resources. Phone and web clients carry the same work contexts
into smaller or desktop layouts.

## Product Purpose

VibePad is a portable workbench for real coding-agent sessions. It connects to
the harnesses and credentials already running on a developer's hosts, preserves
each provider's useful capabilities, and adds a coherent layer for chat, files,
changes, persistent browser previews, drawing, selection, voice, handoffs, and
resource coordination.

Success means a developer can begin or resume work, see what every active
session is doing, inspect the result, point at or draw over the exact surface
that needs attention, speak the intended change, and steer the next action from
the iPad without returning to the host computer.

## Positioning

VibePad is the portable control surface that combines real coding harnesses
across hosts with precise visual, voice, preview, and orchestration workflows.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, capable. VibePad should feel like a focused native workbench
whose controls become obvious through familiarity. Codex, OpenCode, and the host
platform supply behavioral reference points. Drawing, voice, live preview, and
orchestration carry the expressive moments.

## Anti-references

Avoid ambiguous icon-only actions, disconnected control islands, redundant
headers, duplicated browser chrome, permanent overlays that obscure the work,
oversized cards, arbitrary whitespace, fixture content presented as product UI,
and web-shaped controls on native platforms. Standard actions should use
established platform and coding-tool conventions.

## Design Principles

1. **Familiarity creates speed.** Follow the strongest interaction patterns from
   native iPad software, Codex, OpenCode, and established development tools so
   attention stays on the work.
1. **A session owns its context.** Chat, files, changes, previews, browser
   targets, models, activity, and draft feedback remain clearly attached to the
   active harness session.
1. **Current state stays visible.** Running, waiting, unread, disconnected,
   blocked, and completed work should be recognizable at a glance across
   sessions and hosts.
1. **Feedback stays spatial and temporal.** Selection, drawing, screenshots, and
   voice timestamps preserve exactly what the user referenced and when they
   referenced it.
1. **Provider power remains available.** Shared concepts create coherence while
   provider-specific capabilities remain accessible wherever they add value.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA on web and equivalent native support through VoiceOver,
TalkBack, keyboard navigation, scalable text, sufficient contrast,
reduced-motion behavior, and platform-sized touch targets. The landscape iPad
workflow should remain fully usable with touch, keyboard, Apple Pencil, or any
practical combination of those inputs.
